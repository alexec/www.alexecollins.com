title: Stormy Night Support

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Stormy Night is an iPhone app that plays a storm that actually passes. It arrives while you
fall asleep, breaks twice in the small hours, thins out before dawn, and hands the night over
to birds, which keep going until you stop them. Tell it when dawn is and the whole storm
stretches to fit.

## Report a problem

Email me at [alex@alexecollins.com](mailto:alex@alexecollins.com). You do not need an account
anywhere to do it, and I would rather have a short email than nothing.

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. These help:

- the iPhone and the version of iOS on it
- the version of Stormy Night from the App Store listing
- what you were listening through: the phone's speaker, wired headphones, AirPods, a speaker
  over AirPlay
- roughly how far into the night it was, and what you had set as your dawn

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read every
email. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a reply.

## Things that are working as intended

- **Nothing much happens for the first twenty-five minutes.** That is deliberate. You are
  falling asleep in those minutes, and anything with a shape to it would keep you awake.
- **The thunder is quieter than you expect.** Far-off thunder is filtered rather than turned
  down, which is the difference between a storm three miles away and a recording with the
  volume low.
- **It never repeats.** There is no loop to catch. The night is one designed arc with eleven
  layers mixed against it, so the seam you can hear in other apps is not there to find.
- **The birds do not stop.** The storm ends at your dawn and the chorus carries on until you
  stop it, because waking to sudden silence is its own alarm.
- **The silent switch does not silence it.** The app plays as audio rather than as a sound
  effect, which is what lets it keep going with the screen locked.

## If the sound stops

Pulling headphones or AirPods out stops it on purpose, so the phone's speaker does not start
up on its own at three in the morning. Press play again.

Otherwise, check that nothing else on the phone has taken audio over, and that Stormy Night
is the app playing in Control Centre. A call interrupts it and it starts again afterwards
from where the night actually is by then, rather than from where it left off, because the
storm is keyed to the clock rather than to how long it has been playing.

## If the birds arrive at the wrong time

The whole storm is stretched to land at the dawn you set, so moving that time moves
everything before it. Set it before you press play; the picker is there until you do.

## Privacy

Stormy Night collects nothing, asks for no permissions, and makes no network connections.
The [privacy policy](/stormy-night/privacy.html) says what that means in full.
