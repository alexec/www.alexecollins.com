_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Hard Stop lives in your Mac's menu bar. It shows the meeting you are in or the one coming
next, counts down to it, and puts a reminder under the icon before it starts, with a Join
button in it.

## Report a problem

Email me at [alex@alexecollins.com](mailto:alex@alexecollins.com). You do not need an account
anywhere to do it, and I would rather have a short email than nothing.

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. These make it much easier to
find:

- the version of macOS, and the version of Hard Stop from the App Store listing
- which calendar account the meeting came from: iCloud, Google, Exchange, Office 365
- for a Join button that went to the wrong place, the meeting provider and, if you can share
  it, what the link looked like with the meeting id taken out

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read every
email. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a reply.

## Three things that are working as intended

- **All-day entries are missing.** Holidays, someone's leave and a week-long conference are
  not things you can be late for, so they stay out of the list. Turn them on in Settings.
- **Only one calendar shows up.** Hard Stop reads exactly one calendar, the one you pick in
  the popover, because a single choice is a clearer promise than a row of checkboxes.
- **Meetings you declined are hidden.** A meeting you said no to is not your meeting. Settings
  has the switch if you want them back.

If the menu bar says nothing at all, the usual cause is calendar access. Check System
Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars, then pick a calendar again in the popover.

## Privacy

Hard Stop collects nothing. The [privacy policy](/hard-stop/privacy.html) says what that
means in full.
