_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Packing Order turns a destination, two dates and a kind of trip into a packing list you tick
off standing at the wardrobe. It gets better at it the more trips you take. It runs on the
iPhone and the iPad.

## Report a problem

Email me at [alex@alexecollins.com](mailto:alex@alexecollins.com). You do not need an account
anywhere to do it, and I would rather have a short email than nothing.

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. These help:

- the iPhone or iPad, and the version of iOS or iPadOS on it
- the version of Packing Order from the App Store listing
- for a list that came out wrong, the destination, the dates, the kind of trip and how you
  were getting there
- for anything about the weather, whether the header said it was using the forecast or the
  season

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read
everything that comes in. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a
reply.

## Things that are working as intended

- **It keeps suggesting something you never pack.** Swipe it away on two trips and it stops
  suggesting it, as long as you have not packed it more often than you have removed it. It
  also stops after three trips where you left it unticked and never added it yourself.
- **Something you always pack has not appeared yet.** Type it in and it joins the list next
  time for that kind of trip after twice, and for every trip after three times.
- **The weather looks like a guess.** More than ten days out it is one. Apple Weather gives
  ten days; past that the app uses what the season and the latitude usually give, and the
  header says so.
- **No travel adapter for a trip abroad.** It appears only when the plugs there are actually
  different from the ones at home.
- **Quantities stop going up after a week.** After seven days you find a washing machine, so
  the counts cap there rather than sending you with fourteen pairs of socks.

## Getting a trip out of the app

Any open trip shares as plain text, tick boxes and all, into a message, a note or anywhere
else. Getting every trip out at once is on the list and not built yet.

## Privacy

Packing Order collects nothing. Two things go to Apple: the place name you type, and the
destination's coordinate for the forecast. The [privacy policy](/packing-order/privacy.html)
says what that means in full.
