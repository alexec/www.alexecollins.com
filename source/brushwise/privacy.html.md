---
title: Brushwise Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 12 September 2026_

Brushwise does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no
account, no sign-in, no sync and no server of mine. The app makes no network connections at
all, so nothing about your teeth reaches me or anyone else.

## What is kept, and where

Everything the app knows sits on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

- every routine you have run: which stages you finished, when each one started and stopped,
  and whether it counted as the morning or the night
- your settings: which stages are on for each part of the day, the two reminder times,
  sound and haptics, which soundtrack plays, and the date you last put in a fresh
  toothbrush
- which tips you have already been shown, so a new one comes up before a repeat, and which
  achievements you have earned

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Your iPhone's own backup includes the app's storage the
way it includes every other app's, which is between you and Apple rather than anything the
app does.

## Apple Health

**Log to Apple Health** in Settings is off until you turn it on. Turn it on and Brushwise
asks iOS for permission to **write** Tooth Brushing events, and for nothing else. The
request names no read access at all, so there is nothing you could grant that would let the
app read your Health data. Each finished brushing is saved with the time it started, the
time it stopped, and the random identifier the app gave that routine, which is tied to
nothing about you or your phone.

From there it is Health's, under Apple's own rules. Brushwise does not use what it writes
for advertising or marketing and does not pass it to anyone, which is what Apple's
HealthKit terms require. Take the permission back in the Health app (Profile → Apps →
Brushwise), and delete the records there if you want them gone.

## Reminders

Brushwise can remind you twice a day, at times you pick. Each reminder is scheduled on your
iPhone against its own clock, so no server ever knows when you brush or whether you did.
Turn them off in Brushwise's Settings, or in the iOS Settings app.

## The soundtrack

The soundscapes you can play during a routine are made on your phone, sample by sample, as
they play. Brushwise ships no audio files, downloads nothing, and never looks at your music
library.

## What there is none of

No account, no sign-in, no sync, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no in-app
purchases and no third-party SDKs. The app never asks for your location, your contacts,
your photos, your microphone or your camera. It asks for two things and only two: to send
you notifications, and to write to Health. Say no to both and the routine still works
exactly as it does now.
