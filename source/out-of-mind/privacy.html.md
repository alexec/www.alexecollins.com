---
title: Out of Mind Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Out of Mind does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no
account, no sync, and no server of mine. The app has no networking code in it at all.

## What you say

The microphone is open from the moment the app appears, because losing your opening
sentence is the one failure that loses the app to Siri. Your voice becomes words on this
iPhone, using two of Apple's speech engines at once: a slower one whose words are the dump,
and a faster one that only exists to keep the screen level with your mouth. Everything the
fast one produces is thrown away as soon as the slower one settles the same words.

On-device is a requirement here rather than a preference. The slower engine reads your
voice on this iPhone, from a language model iOS downloads once and keeps on the phone. The
fast one is forbidden the network outright, and it is asked beforehand whether it can work
without one: if it cannot, it never starts, and the slower engine keeps the screen level
with your mouth on its own. Neither engine falls back to a server, so there is no case
where your words quietly go to Apple instead of staying here.

The audio is never written down. Nothing is recorded to a file, nothing is kept after the
dump, and nothing is sent anywhere. Out of Mind asks for the microphone and for speech
recognition the first time it needs them, behind a page saying why, and you can withdraw
either in Settings.

It also never listens while you are not looking at it. There is no audio background mode in
the app, so the screen dimming closes the microphone and the phone going away ends the dump.

## The model

Apple's on-device model reads the dump and decides where one job ends and the next begins.
It is part of Apple Intelligence and it runs on this iPhone. Nothing you said is sent to
Apple, and nothing comes to me. On a phone without Apple Intelligence the app splits the
dump with its own rules instead, which also run here, and it carries on without saying
anything about it.

## Reminders

Out of Mind writes each slip into your Reminders and forgets it. It never reads your reminders
back, never edits one, and never deletes one. It asks for full access because iOS has no
write-only permission for Reminders, not because it wants to look.

## What is kept in the app

One file in the app's own storage on the phone: the pile of slips you have said and not yet
dealt with. Each slip holds its title, the clause of the dump it came from kept word for
word, and whatever date, place or people the app lifted out of it. Alongside them is one
sentence summing up the last dump, which is what the morning notification says.

That is the whole of it. A slip leaves the pile into Reminders or into nothing, and there is
no archive, no history and no copy of anything already filed. You can share the pile out as
plain text, one line a slip, which hands over the list you are looking at and keeps nothing.

## Notifications

Two a day, both made on this iPhone: a nudge at five in the afternoon, and a summary the
next morning of what you said. They arrive quietly in Notification Centre rather than as an
alert, and you can promote or silence them there. Nothing is pushed from a server, because
there is no server.

## What there is none of

No account, no sign-in, no iCloud, no sync, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no
in-app purchases, and no subscription.
