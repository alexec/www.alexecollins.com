_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Out of Mind is a pipe from your mouth to Apple Reminders. Hold your thumb on the stone and say
everything that is on your mind. It splits what you said into separate reminders, fills in
the dates, places and people you implied, and writes them into Reminders. Then it goes away.
It is not another to-do app: there is no list, no inbox and no badge.

## Report a problem

Email me at [alex@alexecollins.com](mailto:alex@alexecollins.com). You do not need an account
anywhere to do it, and I would rather have a short email than nothing.

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. These help:

- the iPhone and the version of iOS on it
- the version of Out of Mind from the App Store listing
- whether Apple Intelligence is turned on, under Settings, then Apple Intelligence & Siri
- for a slip that came out wrong, roughly what you said and what it wrote

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read
everything that comes in. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a
reply.

## Things that are working as intended

- **It made a job out of a passing remark.** The bias is deliberate. Nearly everything said
  at five in the afternoon is a thing you have to do, and swiping a slip away costs a thumb
  while recovering a thought you never captured costs another evening.
- **It put a date on something you never dated.** Tomorrow morning is the standing default.
  The docket colours it differently from a time you actually said, so an assumption never
  passes for a decision. Tap the when to change it.
- **It makes no sound.** The microphone is open, so anything the app played would be talking
  over you. Haptics do all of it.
- **There is no history.** Reminders is the store. A slip leaves the pile into Reminders or
  into nothing, and neither way is undoable.
- **The microphone closed when the screen dimmed.** An app that can record while you are not
  looking at it is a different app from this one. Your words are kept; the dump ends.

## If the splitting looks crude

Check Apple Intelligence is turned on. With the model there, the app reads the shape of what
you said. Without it, the app falls back to its own rules and says nothing about it, which
is the right trade at five in the afternoon but does read as blunter. The model needs an
iPhone 15 Pro or later.

## If nothing reaches Reminders

Out of Mind needs full access to Reminders, and iOS offers no smaller one for writing. Check
Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Reminders, then Out of Mind. It writes into your
default list, so if that has been changed the slips are in the new one.

## Privacy

Out of Mind collects nothing, has no network code, and turns your voice into words on the
phone itself. The [privacy policy](/out-of-mind/privacy.html) says what that means in full.
