---
title: Call Your Mom Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Call Your Mom does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What is kept, and where

One file on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

- her phone number, and the identifier of her card in Contacts
- the day and the half hour you chose for the weekly prompt
- the date of each call you made or told it about, and whether it was a call or a tap on "We
  spoke"
- whether you reach her by phone or FaceTime, and whether you have read the first-run sheet

Her name is not in that list and is not stored anywhere. The app says "Mum" on every screen
and in every notification, whatever her contact card is filed under. Not keeping the name at
all is what makes that impossible to get wrong later.

The app has no account, no iCloud sync and no backup of its own, and it sends nothing
anywhere. That also means the counter does not survive deleting the app. It is the trade the
app makes: it knows one thing about you, and it only knows it here.

## Contacts

You pick her through iOS's own contact picker, which runs outside the app. Call Your Mom never
asks for permission to your address book and never sees anybody else in it. What comes back is
her phone number and the identifier of her card. Not her name, not her email, not her
photograph.

## Calls

Tapping Call opens the Phone app or FaceTime with her number in it. While the app is still on
screen it watches for a call connecting, so that a call which rang out does not reset the
counter; after that it judges by how long you were gone. It cannot see who you called and it
never reads your call history.

## Notifications

The weekly prompt and the catch-up nudge are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. They
are sent time-sensitive so they get through Focus. Nothing goes through a server, and there is
no push.

## Network

Call Your Mom makes no network connections. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts,
no in-app purchases, and no third-party services.
