---
title: Sleeper Train Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Sleeper Train does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app does

Sleeper Train plays a designed overnight train journey, mixed live on your iPhone from audio
files built into the app. That is the whole of it. It asks for no permissions at all: no
microphone, no location, no notifications, no contacts, no photos, no health data, no motion.

The arrival time it suggests comes from a sunrise worked out from the date and your device's
time zone. It does not use Core Location, so the app never knows where you are.

## What is kept

One thing, on your phone: whether you have read the first-run sheet, so that it appears once
and then stops. The time you ask to arrive lives only while the app is running and is not
written anywhere.

## Network

Sleeper Train makes no network connections. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts,
no in-app purchases, and no third-party services. Every sound is inside the app, so nothing is
fetched to make it work and nothing is reported back.
