---
title: Hold Still Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Hold Still does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no
account, no upload, no analytics, and no server of mine. The app makes no network
connections at all.

## Your photographs

Hold Still takes one photograph a day and keeps it on the phone. The frames are plain JPEGs
in the app's own storage, one per day, named for the day they were taken. Nothing is copied
anywhere, and nothing is sent.

The app asks for the camera the first time you use it, behind the page that explains what it
does, and you can withdraw it in Settings. That is the only permission it needs to work.

The app never looks at what is in a photograph. There is no face detection, no subject
recognition and no automatic straightening. Yesterday's frame is drawn faintly over the live
preview so you can line the shot up by eye, which is the whole of the help it gives.

Hold Still never asks for your location, so nothing in the app knows where a photograph was
taken. Each frame is re-encoded when it is saved, which leaves the camera's own metadata
behind rather than writing it into the file.

## The reel

The reel is stitched from your frames on the phone, into a temporary file, whenever a day is
added. It is rebuilt rather than kept, and iOS may clear it at any time.

Sharing the reel hands a video file to the iOS share sheet, and where it goes from there is
your choice and the choice of whichever app you send it to. Hold Still is not involved after
that, and it never shares anything on its own.

## The daily reminder

The daily nudge is a local notification scheduled by the app on this iPhone at the time you
chose. Nothing is pushed from a server, because there is no server. You can turn it off in
the app, or silence it in Settings.

## What else is kept

Your preferences, in the phone's own settings store: whether the reminder is on and when,
how strongly the ghost shows through, which camera you use, and whether the front camera is
mirrored. The streak is counted from the frames themselves, along with the longest run you
have managed.

## What there is none of

No account, no sign-in, no iCloud, no sync, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no
in-app purchases, and no subscription. The app is free and stays free.
